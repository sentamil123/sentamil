Nexto Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This page is an initial privacy notice for Nexto. Before public release, the operator must add a verified contact email, hosting/storage providers, retention periods, account deletion process, and any analytics or advertising disclosures. Do not submit this draft as a final Play Store policy until those details are completed.

Information we may process

When enabled in the deployed version, Nexto may process account details such as username and email, password hashes, profile details, posts, media, comments, likes, follows, saved posts, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, display social content and interactions, operate the service, prevent abuse, and maintain security.

Storage and security

Data is stored by the service's configured hosting and database providers. Passwords should be stored as hashes. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing and visibility

Content posted to a social profile may be visible to other users. Do not post information you do not want to share. The final policy must describe any third-party processors and legal disclosures accurately.

Retention and account deletion

The production release must provide a clear way to request account deletion and deletion of associated data, subject to lawful retention requirements. The operator must publish a working contact method here before release.

Children

Nexto is not intended for children under the minimum age applicable in their country. The operator must define and enforce age eligibility before public release.
